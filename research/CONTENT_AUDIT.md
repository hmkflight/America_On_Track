# Content audit and audience architecture

## Inventory
21 published pages captured through the public WordPress API, plus homepage HTML, page sitemap, and event PDF. Core navigation contains separate biographies, many overlapping tobacco pages, highly verbose program prose, dense awards lists and undated counters. There is no dedicated public resources page in the fetched page inventory; the new Resources route curates existing forms, documents and archives.

## Main audience needs
| Audience | Immediate question | Primary path | Deeper content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teens / students | What can I join? | Emerging Leaders / teen interest form | Skills, scholarships, participation questions |
| Parents / caregivers | Is there support for our family? | Brighter Futures / contact | Five components, availability, program fit |
| Schools / educators | What can you bring to our school? | Programs audience filter | Fitness, nutrition, prevention formats |
| Mentors / volunteers | How can I help? | Get involved | Adult mentor, teen, general volunteer forms |
| Donors / funders | Why trust and support this work? | Impact / Donate | Dated evidence, history, leadership, current report inquiry |
| Health / civic partners | What changes at community level? | Tobacco-Free Communities | Youth access vs smoke-free environments, policy milestones |
| Event participants | When, where, how? | Golf | Schedule, official registration, sponsorship, dinner, auction |

## Consolidation
- Three tobacco pages become one substantive program with separately explained youth-access and secondhand-smoke work.
- Founders and three board groups become one accessible leadership route; original biographies remain linked for full depth.
- Our Story, Awards, and results history no longer repeat institutional introduction. About communicates mission; History curates milestones; Impact explains evidence.
- Volunteer and Contact duplicate three forms. The redesign gives each a clear audience and purpose, with direct official destinations.
- Long generic quotations, unsupported comparative claims, old public-health numbers, redundant mission blocks, and repetitive testimonial paragraphs are removed.
- Historical impact totals are dated; duplicated contacts are clearly distinguished from individuals.
- Resources is an organized index, not a new claim of clinical/public-health resources.
- Historical funder listings are treated as archived institutional support, not a new "current sponsors" logo wall.
- Golf is discoverable on Home, Get Involved, footer and Resources, while remaining separate from the permanent organizational story.

## Layering
30-second overview on Home → audience-specific program explorer → concise program pages → details disclosures and original archive links. Six program detail routes retain differences in audience, delivery and outcomes; no identical card grid.
