# Facts carried into the redesign

Verified against the public site on October 2, 2026. "Published" below means first-party evidence, not independent confirmation of every current operational detail.

- Founded in 1995 by Terry Thompson and Claire Braeburn, after research into Orange County needs (Our Story, both biographies).
- Mission: build youth leaders, support families, strengthen communities through life-transforming programs. Vision includes reducing social inequities and health disparities.
- Terry is published as President and Co-Founder; Claire as Executive Director and Co-Founder.
- Independent 501(c)(3); EIN 33-0724044. 600 W. Santa Ana Blvd., Suite 710, Santa Ana, CA 92701. Phone 714-531-7144, fax 714-531-7773, PR@AmericaOnTrack.org.
- Emerging Leaders for Civic Engagement: after-school program for grades 4–12, public speaking, coaching, STEM exposure, arts, fitness/nutrition, prevention, volunteering/civic engagement. Scholarships are described, but no amounts or guarantee of current awards are asserted.
- Brighter Futures for Children of Prisoners: established 2004, free five-component program: STEAM, CSUF summer college camp, qualified/trained mentors, special events, wraparound family support and books.
- Fitness: structured PE, Fitness Fun Zone active recess, teacher/parent training, stencils, policies, measurement and staff development. 21 schools since 2006 is a historical total.
- Nutrition: youth/adult classes, shopping tours, demonstrations, resident leadership, policies and community booths.
- Drug prevention: multi-week school programming plus one-hour youth/adult presentations. No current epidemiological claims copied.
- Tobacco work: prevention since 1997; youth access and secondhand-smoke policy are distinct project strands. Dedicated policy page includes July 2023 Buena Park milestone.
- Current published Board page lists 10 Directors, 4 Honorary Board members, 4 Advisory Board members. All preserved in content.ts, with source timestamp context.
- 2019 results: 5,597 classes/presentations/trainings/events and 259,452 duplicated service contacts. These are explicitly historical; not unique people or 2026 results.
