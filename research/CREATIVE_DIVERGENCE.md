# Creative divergence — 7 October 2026

Three directions evaluated before new implementation against specificity, originality, clarity, authentic imagery, motion, mobile, memorability, feasibility and distance from the retired build.

## Signal / Response — rejected
A high-contrast crimson/black civic broadcast: kinetic headlines, waveform transitions, radio-like program tuning, large raw photographic crops. Distinct but the broadcast metaphor puts the organization in the position of speaking at people; too little room for mentoring's patient human dimension.

## The Living Commons — rejected
A cobalt/cyan procedural garden of community infrastructure with branching forms and pan-and-zoom content. Strong systems story, but too close to the prior connection explorer and risks implying quantitative relationships unsupported by evidence. Navigation would need a second plain-language layer.

## Room to Become — selected
An open, lilac architectural environment. A series of dimensional apertures makes real photographs feel like spaces being opened, not charity advertisements. An expanding program gallery gives each area room; the visual action of opening is also the navigation action. Wide, low-contrast grotesk typography meets intimate italic serif; aubergine and lilac replace the previous warm editorial palette. Archive folios and a governance assembly extend the same space-making logic.

Big idea: People will remember America On Track because its website physically makes room for the potential already inside people—opening spaces for mentoring, leadership, learning and healthier living.

Why it won: the organization does more than teach individual skills; it creates supportive conditions around children, families, schools and public spaces. Spatial depth makes this structural idea tangible, while normal links and semantic content keep it usable. CSS perspective is sufficient: no WebGL payload or decorative spinning object is needed.
